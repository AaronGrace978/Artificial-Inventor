HUMAN AUTHORITY
◎

Capability does not grant permission.

This application cannot operate laboratory equipment, run generated programs, modify its verifier, or deploy self-proposed improvements. Human values must be supplied through human decisions and explicit constraints.

01

Correctness beyond tests

NO GENERAL GUARANTEE

Design limits, assumptions, analytical checks, and sensitivity analysis expose a stated operating envelope. Extrapolation remains unvalidated.

02

Every possible model error

NOT EXHAUSTIVE

Separate calculations can detect disagreement. Shared assumptions can fail together; agreement is not proof that the physical model is right.

03

Every conceivable claim

EVIDENCE IS SCOPED

Recorded predictions carry an evidence level and a falsifying experiment. Empirical claims require measurement; arbitrary claims are not automatically verified.

04

Every specification loophole

UNKNOWN GAPS REMAIN

Fixed schemas, hard numerical constraints, and candidate budgets constrain the search. Passing them does not establish that the specification captures every intention.

05

Determining human values

HUMAN DECISION

The operator sets the objective and constraints. Optimization cannot establish moral authority, decide whose interests count, or resolve social disagreement.

06

Unlimited self-improvement

NO SELF-DEPLOYMENT

Changes to the research system require a new version, offline evaluation, external review, and a separate release. Unlimited safe recursive improvement is unproven.
